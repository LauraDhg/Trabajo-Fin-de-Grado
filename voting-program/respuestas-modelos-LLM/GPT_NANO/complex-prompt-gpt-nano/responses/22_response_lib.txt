- Potential for integer underflows when subtracting governance points without safeguards.
- Insufficient validation of user inputs (like proposal titles/descriptions) for size and security implications.
- Lack of explicit authorization checks beyond basic owner or signer, leaving potential attack surface if owner keys are compromised or misconfigured.

- Critical functions can panic due to unwrap() calls on fallible operations, risking contract failure during execution.
